## Prompt final

Refine the supplied school library crest into a polished professional logo for the Ruang Pustaka website. Preserve the exact identity and arrangement: cyan blue shield with gently concave top corners and pointed bottom, dark navy outline and white inset border, central white open book with navy pages/lines, three upright blue yellow green books behind, golden rice stalk on left, green cotton stalk with white blossoms on right crossing below book. Preserve ALL exact Indonesian text: top curved 'PERPUSTAKAAN', centered 'SMP NEGERI 26', bottom curved 'MEMBACA • BELAJAR • BERKARYA'. Correctly spelled crisp beautifully spaced bold sans serif lettering, balanced symmetrical composition, clean consistent strokes, flat solid vector-like colors, remove fuzziness and uneven edges and paper texture. High resolution square transparent PNG, shield fills about 94% of canvas height with minimal equal padding, fully transparent outside shield, opaque blue inside. One finished logo only, no mockup, no extra text, no drop shadow, no white background. Keep faithful to reference rather than redesigning the symbols.
